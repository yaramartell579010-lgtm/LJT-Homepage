I am a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Prior to this, I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning. Specific research interests include:
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
